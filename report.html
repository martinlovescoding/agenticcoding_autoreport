Conventions

  • A row is one interaction. Only rows that survived cleaning are counted, and the ledger above accounts for every row that did not.
  • A missing engagement score is excluded from every average and is never read as a zero. Nothing is imputed and no value is invented.
  • Dates are read day-first. A numeric date whose first field is above 12 is treated as month-first, because that reading is the only unambiguous one.
  • Colour carries channel identity, so a channel keeps its hue when it changes rank between periods. Each chart also carries its values as text — on the marks where they fit, and in the table view beneath every chart.
