How to read this

  • One row is one interaction. Only rows that survived cleaning are counted, and the ledger beside this accounts for every row that did not.
  • A missing score is not a zero. Those rows count toward the volume and are excluded from every average. Nothing is imputed and no value is invented.
  • Dates are read day-first. A numeric date whose first field is above 12 is read month-first, because that reading is the only unambiguous one.
  • Colour is identity, not rank. A channel keeps its hue when it changes place between periods, and every chart carries its values as text — in the table view beneath it, and on the marks where they fit.
